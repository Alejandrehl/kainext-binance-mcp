# Glossary — terms with an interpretive reading

Not just definitions: what each number *means* when you see it.

- **Funding rate** (perpetual futures): periodic payment between longs and shorts that
  pegs the perp to spot. Positive = longs pay shorts (bullish positioning); sustained
  > 0.05%/8h = crowded longs, squeeze fuel. Negative = shorts pay = fear, capitulation
  when deeply negative.
- **Open interest (OI)**: total outstanding futures contracts. Rising OI + rising price =
  leveraged rally (fragile); falling OI on a dip = deleveraging (healthier). OI flushes
  mark local extremes.
- **BTC dominance**: BTC's share of total crypto market cap. Rising = risk-off within
  crypto (money hides in BTC); falling fast = alt speculation phase, historically late-cycle.
- **Mayer Multiple**: price / 200-day MA. Zone indicator: <0.8 historically deep value,
  >2.4 euphoria. Useless in between.
- **ATH drawdown**: % below all-time high. Locates you in the cycle band (see
  `kb://frameworks/cycle-analysis`).
- **Fear & Greed Index**: composite retail-sentiment proxy (volatility, momentum, social,
  dominance, volume). Contrarian at extremes (<15, >85); noise in the middle.
- **FDV (fully diluted valuation)**: price × max supply. The honest valuation for tokens
  with large unlocks pending; market cap alone flatters them.
- **Unlock schedule**: calendar on which team/investor tokens become sellable. Structural
  sell pressure — check before holding any altcoin.
- **TVL (total value locked)**: capital parked in a protocol. Adoption proxy, NOT revenue —
  and not value captured by the token (see `kb://frameworks/token-value`).
- **Basis**: futures price minus spot. Wide positive basis = leveraged bullishness (cash-
  and-carry becomes attractive); negative = stress.
- **Hashrate**: total mining compute. Long-run security/health signal; short-run moves are
  noise. Sharp sustained drops = miner stress (forced selling risk).
- **Realized volatility**: standard deviation of actual returns over a window. Sizing
  input: a 60%-vol asset needs a position ~4x smaller than a 15%-vol one for equal risk.
- **Max drawdown**: worst peak-to-trough loss over a period. The number to rehearse
  emotionally BEFORE buying, at position size (see `kb://discipline` rule 2).
- **DCA (dollar-cost averaging)**: fixed buys on a fixed schedule, unconditionally. The
  behavioral edge available to everyone (see `kb://discipline` rule 1).
- **Walk-forward validation**: backtesting where parameters are fit on past windows and
  measured on unseen future windows. The minimum honest standard — in-sample backtests
  are marketing (`kb://research/no-edge`).
