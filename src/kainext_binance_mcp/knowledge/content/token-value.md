# Token value framework — does the token capture the value?

The most expensive confusion in crypto: **a successful project does not imply a valuable
token.** Adoption headlines pump narratives; only value *capture* sustains prices. Apply
this checklist before holding any non-BTC asset.

## The five questions
1. **What does holding the token actually entitle you to?**
   Fee share? Staking yield from real revenue? Governance over a treasury? Burn tied to
   usage? Or... nothing but resale? If the honest answer is "resale", you hold a
   narrative, not an asset.
2. **Protocol revenue vs valuation.** Compare actual protocol revenue (not volume, not
   TVL) with fully-diluted valuation (FDV). Revenue of $10-50M/quarter against an FDV of
   $10B+ means the market prices decades of flawless growth. Common in "adopted"
   projects whose token captures none of the adoption.
3. **Supply schedule.** Max supply, % circulating, unlock calendar. Large scheduled
   unlocks (team/investor tranches) are structural sell pressure that no narrative
   outruns. Grants and emissions given away for free WILL be sold.
4. **Is the "institutional adoption" monetized?** Pilots, MOUs and testnets are not
   revenue. Count only integrations that pay fees at scale, today.
5. **Competitive moat.** Who else provides this? Is share measured by a verifiable
   metric, or by the project's own marketing? Fast-growing challengers compress fees for
   everyone.

## Red flags that end the analysis
- Value-capture mechanism "coming in a future version" — for years.
- Metrics that only the team publishes, refuted by third-party data.
- Token needed for nothing the protocol's customers actually do.

## The clean comparison: BTC
Bitcoin does not have the value-capture question: the asset IS the product (scarce,
neutral, bearer settlement). That is why it is the default core holding and the benchmark
every altcoin thesis must beat — an altcoin must promise MORE than BTC's risk-adjusted
return to justify its extra layer of risk (execution + competition + value capture).
